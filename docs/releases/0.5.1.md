## QueueScope 0.5.1

Fixes the failure inbox staying on the previous scope when you switch queues.

- The inbox follows the selected queue by default, including the offline demo.
- An explicit **All queues** option keeps connection-wide error grouping available.
- Switching scope resets scan pagination and comparisons, and ignores late results from the previous scan.
- Counts, search, expanded groups and workspace labels stay consistent with the active scope.

Download the ZIP and move QueueScope.app to Applications, or use **Check for Updates** in the app. Requires macOS 14 or later and supports Apple Silicon and Intel. Targets BullMQ 5.77.x.

Signed with Developer ID and notarized by Apple. SHA-256 is included in the attached SHA256SUMS file.
