## QueueScope 0.6.0

Work across independent windows, recover from dropped connections, and manage scheduled jobs and queue history with more control.

- Open independent workspaces with their own connection, queue selection and refresh lifecycle.
- Reconnect automatically with visible retry timing while keeping cached queue data available.
- Preview upcoming repeat schedules and remove modern or legacy schedulers. Choose a preview timezone; recorded schedule timezones take precedence by default, with your Mac’s timezone as the fallback.
- Clean completed or failed jobs by age and count with connection-specific confirmation and read-only safeguards.
- Discover queues more easily on first launch and import queue catalogs from Node and Next.js integrations.

Custom repeat strategies require worker code and remain unavailable for future-run projection. Timezone overrides affect previews, not the worker’s schedule.

Download the ZIP and move QueueScope.app to Applications, or use **Check for Updates** in the app. Requires macOS 14 or later and supports Apple Silicon and Intel. Targets BullMQ 5.77.x.
